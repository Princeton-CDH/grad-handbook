Our range of curricular and co-curricular offerings has expanded over the years. Some students hold multiple CDH positions in a single academic year.

{{< figure src="images/grad_programs.png" alt="Color-coded bar chart showing the number of students in each program per academic year.">}}

There is no one right way to sequence the workshops, classes, and fellowships offered both by us and our campus partners like [Digital Scholarship](https://library.princeton.edu/digital-scholarship) (DiScho) and [Research Computing](https://researchcomputing.princeton.edu/learn/workshops-live-training). The diagram below suggests some possible pathways that PhD students might want to follow: from introductory classes, to project-based learning, to advanced training, and finally capstone presentations.

![](https://princeton-cdh.github.io/grad-handbook/cdh-grad-pathways.svg)
